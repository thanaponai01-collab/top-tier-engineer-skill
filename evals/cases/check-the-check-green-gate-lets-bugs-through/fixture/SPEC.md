# Shipping and order totals

1. Standard shipping is $4.99. Orders with a subtotal of $50.00 or more ship free.
2. Parcels heavier than 20 kg add a $15.00 heavy-item fee, on top of any shipping cost.
3. Express delivery adds $7.50, also on orders that ship free.
4. Sales tax is 8% of the subtotal only, rounded to the cent. Shipping is not taxed.
