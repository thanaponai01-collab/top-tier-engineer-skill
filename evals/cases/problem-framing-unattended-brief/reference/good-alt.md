I wrote the brief to BRIEF.md so Monday's builder can start from it. Highlights:

The real goal is that the six salespeople no longer retype orders by hand, and the shipping errors from
mistyped quantities end. "An app" is just the means.

The assumption I'm least sure of, and the one that matters most: I've assumed the tool creates
orders in Odoo as drafts for a salesperson to check and confirm, rather than posting them on its own.
Everything else depends on that, so it's the one question for Maria when she's back.

Instead of "fast and easy", the brief has numbers: an emailed order of up to 20 lines appears as a
draft within 1 minute of arriving; checking it takes a salesperson at most 2 minutes; a line the tool
isn't sure about (low confidence, unknown SKU, an unreadable PDF) is highlighted and can't be confirmed
until fixed; if Odoo is unavailable, drafts queue and nothing is lost.

Out of scope: phone orders, a customer portal, automatic posting. Assumptions and their cost if wrong are
in a table at the end of the brief.
