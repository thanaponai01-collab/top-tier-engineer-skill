The least moving parts (one table) that can meet launch behavior is a synchronous refund call in the existing order service, with durable idempotency and refund status in the current DB. The distinct alternative is a transactional refund + outbox record followed by a worker call to the provider; it tolerates slow/retried delivery, at the cost of a queue, worker and eventual status.

Choose the synchronous option for the stated 20/day launch because there is no response-time target; confirm provider latency before relying on it. The refund idempotency record and any external status/response contract are hard to reverse once consumers depend on them.
