Use whenever someone wants to know that a change really works or a task is really done, not just
hear that it is: making sure a change works before committing, doubting a fix or another agent's
"done", asking for proof instead of an opinion, asking whether the tests would notice a broken
result, or asking to verify or review a diff, commit, branch or PR. Also for creating or
maintaining a project's /verify skill or VERIFY.md. Its helper shows a check failing on a broken
product and passing on the fixed one, and keeps that record, which checking by hand does not.
Not for just running tests, building features or explaining code.
